## Task 2 — implementation (green)

In `src/entities/enemies/walker.ts` only:

- Import `mergeGeometries` from `three/addons/utils/BufferGeometryUtils.js`.
- Replace `WALKER_COLOR` with `WALKER_CAP_COLOR = 0xc4362f` and
  `WALKER_STEM_COLOR = 0xf2e2c4`.
- Widen the field type to `THREE.Mesh<THREE.BufferGeometry, THREE.MeshLambertMaterial>`.
- Module-level `createWalkerGeometry()` helper building, in world units (`TILE_SIZE` per
  tile), centred on the origin:

  | part | w × h × d (tiles) | centre Y (tiles) | spans Y |
  |------|-------------------|------------------|---------|
  | cap  | 1.00 × 0.45 × 1.00 | +0.275 | +0.05 … +0.50 |
  | stem | 0.55 × 0.60 × 0.55 | −0.20  | −0.50 … +0.10 |

  Paint a flat `color` attribute on each box, `mergeGeometries([cap, stem])` with
  `useGroups` at its default `false`, then dispose both sources.
- Material: `new THREE.MeshLambertMaterial({ vertexColors: true })` — no `color`, so it
  stays white and does not tint the vertex colors.
- Update the top-of-file comment: "gray-box mesh" → the mushroom description, keeping the
  hitbox-decoupling sentence.
